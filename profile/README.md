## What IronCAD Is

IronCAD is a mechanical modeler where you pull shapes onto a scene instead of rebuilding every solid from an empty sketch. IronCAD drag and drop modeling drops blocks, cylinders, holes, and catalog items into place, then direct edit handles resize a face or feature while the scene stays in front of you. IronCAD catalog part reuse keeps fasteners, brackets, and company hardware on the same design board, so a live review can swap a bolt or stretch a plate without leaving the model. The scene holds parts and assemblies together, which suits concept machines that change shape several times in one sitting.

Concept teams open IronCAD when the goal is a clear form quickly, then a structured model the shop can still read. The TriBall and handle edits move geometry in the view, and catalogs keep standard parts ready beside the scene. IronCAD lets you mix quick shape edits with catalog hardware on one design board.

## Practical Notes for IronCAD

Keep company fasteners in an IronCAD catalog so reviews do not pause to remodel a bolt. Drop the main volumes first, then add holes and hardware once the envelope is agreed. Name scene parts when the model grows past a single mechanism.

IronCAD pays off when the scene stays the place where shape and standard parts meet. Use drag and drop for the first form, then handles for the edits that come up in the room.
